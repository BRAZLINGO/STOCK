The setup, step by step▾
01

Spot the divergence

The scan finds swing bottoms and keeps the pairs where price printed a lower bottom while RSI printed a higher bottom. Under 30 is oversold, over 70 overbought.

02

Mark the resistance

The swing high between those two bottoms is the level that has to break. Override it in any row if your chart reads it differently.

03

Set the stop by ATR

Stop sits ATR × multiplier below the breakout level, so volatility sets the distance.

04

Size to the RPT

Risk per trade = total risk ÷ 50. On ₹1,00,000 that's ₹2,000. Quantity = RPT ÷ risk per share.
